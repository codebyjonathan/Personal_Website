A STUDENT BUILDER FROM MENDOTA

My path

I learn by making things that matter.

I’m a self-taught programmer with more than four years of development experience. At Coalinga College and Lemoore College, I’m pursuing associate degrees in agriculture science, computer information systems, liberal arts, and math and science.

Since 2022, I’ve led programming for MESA. My teams have earned back-to-back national MESA NEDC championships, and I’ve contributed to student mental-health apps including Safe Space and FentaKNOW.

I also co-founded FIRST Tech Challenge team #31174, helping grow it from 3 to 26 members and contributing 200+ hours to technical STEM outreach. I’ve tutored students at Mendota Elementary and completed 450+ hours of community service.
